Starlight Meadow: Privacy Policy

Android app · Effective date: 25 November 2026

Starlight Meadow is a single-player offline game. We (the developer) do not run any servers and do not collect, store, or receive your personal information.

Developer Infinite Creation Studio (Abbey-Rose Kelley), Australia
Contact rosemusk012@gmail.com

1. Information the game keeps on your device

The game saves your best score, your progress (the realm to continue from and acorns found) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, screen shake) on your device using local storage. This never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and app-set ID;
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate, coarse location such as country or city);
  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interaction data (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic/crash data from the SDK.

Google uses this information as described in its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change this choice at any time from the consent message when it is offered.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account. We do not sell personal information. The game has no chat, no user-generated content, and no in-app purchases.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content in the game is limited to the "General" (family-friendly) rating. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you have a privacy concern, contact us at the email above.

5. Security and retention

We hold no personal data. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and secured under Google's policies linked above.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
