Privacy Policy for Violet at Twilight

Android app · Effective date: 25 November 2026

Violet at Twilight is a game by Abbey-Rose Kelley, trading as Infinite Creation Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Android app.

1. Information the game itself collects

The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Game progress and settings (such as scores, unlocked levels and sound on/off) are saved only on your device, and are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data. We do not receive this information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (or an app-set ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google may use this information to show personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your choices and the law where you live. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google AdMob data disclosure: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads. The game itself works offline.

4. Children

This game is intended for players aged 13 and over. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with the app's store listing, with a new effective date.
